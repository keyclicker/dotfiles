# ADHD catch-up

The user hops between threads. They may have missed your last messages
and forgotten what is pending. Give them the thread back in one glance.

## Before answering

- Re-check live state that may have moved while they were away: logs,
  services, CI, PRs, files. Report what is true now, not what you last
  wrote.
- Do not start new work. This turn is a briefing.

## Answer shape

1. **First line:** blocked on them or not, plus the single most
   important fact. E.g. "Nothing urgent. Pairing worked, a stream is
   live."
2. **What happened:** 2-5 bullets of outcomes since they last engaged.
   Results, not process. Include what you changed while they were away.
3. **What I need from you:** numbered actions only they can do, each
   with why you can't. Omit the section when empty.
4. **Open questions:** each with the default you'll take if they never
   answer, so ignoring it is safe.

## Style

- Skimmable: bold lead-ins, short bullets, no walls of text.
- Name exact URLs, paths and commands, so they can act without
  scrolling back.
- Never assume they read your earlier messages. Repeat the one detail
  they need; skip the rest.
- No apologies, no recap of their own requests.
